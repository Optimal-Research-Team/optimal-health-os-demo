Why it matters

Apolipoprotein B — count of atherogenic particles. The single best lipid predictor of cardiovascular risk.

Responds to saturated-fat intake, soluble fibre, weight, and — strongly — to statins or ezetimibe when lifestyle alone isn't enough. Track it after any diet change; it moves within 6–12 weeks.

This is context to bring to your doctor — not medical advice. Optimal Health OS describes trends and targets; it doesn't diagnose or prescribe.
